Gameplay & Mechanics

End-to-End Construction Dynamics

Construction Simulation

The Building Cycle: Materials, Mixing & Laying

Players manage authentic construction materials (cement, gravel, sand, rebar, and concrete blocks). Through specialized mixing and pouring stations, you prepare batches and lay down foundations, slabs, and walls step by step.

Mobile-First UI

Touch Controls & Dynamic Orientation

Features on-screen responsive touch controls. Smoothly adapts between portrait and landscape modes without resetting player progress.

Cloud Saves

Persistence & Leaderboards with Supabase

Integrated authentication and cloud storage to safely sync game progress, player rank, and level achievements across devices.

Engineering

WebGL Optimization for Instant Web Play

Brotli (.br) Compression

The full Unity WebGL build is compressed via Brotli and served using custom _headers on Netlify for rapid mobile downloads.

Service Worker & PWA Cache

Includes a dedicated Service Worker that caches WASM binaries and assets for instant re-opening once installed to a home screen.

Optimized Sprites & Audio

Custom trimming scripts for spritesheets and low-overhead Web Audio triggers responsive feedback for mixing, pouring, and tool actions.
