What it does

Your team informed, without lifting a finger

Sales information reaches whoever needs it, right where they already spend the day: WhatsApp.

Arrives on its own, twice a day

In the morning and in the afternoon, every rep gets their summary on WhatsApp without asking. They start and end the day knowing exactly where they stand.

Their numbers, not everyone's

Every message is personalized: their sales, their target and their progress percentage. No one sees data that isn't theirs.

Report to the manager

At end of day, the manager gets a consolidated view: team ranking, totals and trend. Everything at a glance.

On the WhatsApp Business API

It uses the WhatsApp your team already has in their pocket. Nothing to install, nothing new to learn.

Scheduled and automatic

It goes out at the time you define and runs on its own, every day. Set it once and forget about it.

Zero data entry, zero copy-paste

The numbers come straight from your sales data source. No one builds reports by hand or forwards screenshots: repetitive work is gone.
