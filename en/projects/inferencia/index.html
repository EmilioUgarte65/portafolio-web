Methodology & Findings

4 Core Experimental Discoveries

Finding 1 · Hessian Quantization

GPTQ Solves INT4 and INT3 Completely

Naive uniform quantization collapses catastrophically. By minimizing activation error ‖(W−Q)X‖² using Hessian H = XXᵀ and column-by-column compensation, INT4 reaches 1.01× (indistinguishable from FP32) and INT3 reaches 1.15×, allowing 7B models in ~2.6 GB.

Finding 2 · The INT2 Wall

Why 2-bit Diverges

At 2 bits, classic GPTQ diverges (22,820× error) because the feedback loop amplifies error residuals. Introducing error-feedback clipping at ±2.5σ stabilizes the loop, jumping from 288× to 12.45× at pure 2 bits.

Finding 3 · Orthogonal Stacking

Low-Rank Residual Patches (HADA-LR)

Correcting quantization residuals in activation space with a low-rank approximation drops error drastically: rank-16 reaches 1.54× at 2.67 bits and rank-32 reaches 1.14× at 3.33 bits.
