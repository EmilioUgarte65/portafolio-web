In plain words

What is it and what is it for?

What is a CRM?

A CRM (Customer Relationship Management system) is where everything about each customer lives: conversations, contact details, sales opportunities and follow-ups.

The problem

When every rep handles WhatsApp from their own phone, there is no shared history, nobody knows who replied, prices are quoted from memory and management cannot see how many conversations turn into sales.

The solution

Nexo brings every chat into a shared inbox, links each contact to their ERP record (the sales and inventory system) and lets AI handle the repetitive questions. The rep decides in which chat AI steps in and which one they handle.

Step by step

How a conversation is handled

A message arrives

Through WhatsApp or the website widget. It shows up instantly in the inbox and is assigned to a rep, manually or by workload.

AI or the rep replies

If the AI agent is on for that chat, it answers with the company's knowledge and tools; otherwise the rep replies, helped by canned responses.

The ERP is queried

It identifies the customer, prices by tax ID (RFC) and price group, checks stock and sends the quote or invoice as a PDF.

Close and measure

The conversation is resolved, the opportunity moves along the pipeline (the stage-by-stage sales funnel) and reports show response times and conversions.

What it does

Your WhatsApp sales force, organized

Every rep works on WhatsApp, but everything is connected to the ERP and an AI brain.

Centralized multi-agent

Each rep connects their own WhatsApp number and works from a shared inbox or a desktop app. Assignment, statuses, priority, internal notes and bulk actions, with each rep's portfolio kept separate (commissions intact).

AI per chat

Turn the assistant on or off in each conversation. The rep decides when AI steps in and when they take over.

Price verification

The right price for each customer, by tax ID and price group, instantly.

Live ERP connection

Checks prices and stock per store, identifies the customer and reads and writes data in Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central through its official web services.

Quotes and invoices

Generates and sends PDF quotes and invoices through the chat, in the official format and with the right price.

Voice, images and photos

Listens to voice notes, understands customer photos and sends product photos by SKU (each item's code).

No-code agents and flows

An orchestrator agent routes each conversation to specialist sub-agents. Flows are built by dragging blocks: trigger, message, ask for data, condition, tool, AI, transfer and end.

Knowledge with RAG

RAG (retrieval-augmented generation): upload company documents and the agent searches them before answering, so replies use real information instead of guesses.

Campaigns

Bulk sends with approved WhatsApp templates, rate limiting and tracking of sent, delivered and read.

Pipeline and reports

Kanban board of opportunities and BI (business intelligence) reports: conversions, response times and performance per agent.

Monitoring and evals

Every AI response is traced, and evals (automated agent tests) check quality before publishing.

Data connections

REST, SQL Server, MySQL, PostgreSQL or Google Sheets, with parameterized read-only queries.

Under the hood

Built for production

Real time in a single process

Node.js with Express and Socket.IO (WebSocket: an open connection so messages appear instantly). One process serves the app, the API (the interface for programs) and the WebSocket.

Installable web app

PWA (a web app that installs like a native app) built with React, Vite and Tailwind; the flow editor uses React Flow.

Multi-tenant with roles

Everything is scoped per organization: each company sees only its own data, with ownership checks on every resource. JWT sessions (signed tokens), bcrypt passwords and admin, supervisor and agent roles.

Swappable AI with guardrails

OpenAI, Gemini or Claude, with each company's key stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM). Routing between a cheap and a powerful model, plus filters that block profanity and redact personal data. Reuses CORA as its brain.

Official WhatsApp

Ready for Meta's official API (WhatsApp Cloud API) or a provider, with a webhook (an automatic notice for each message) that responds instantly. The WhatsApp module is swappable.

ERP and desktop

Business Central integration through official web services with least-privilege access. Electron desktop app with native notifications and local history; each customer belongs to the rep who captured them.
