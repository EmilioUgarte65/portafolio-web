Who is Emilio Fonseca and what is his technical background?

Emilio Fonseca is a full-stack developer and creative technologist based in Tampico, Tamaulipas, Mexico. He focuses on the convergence of deep software engineering and high-fidelity interactive design: WebGL/WASM physics games, in-browser multimedia authoring suites, production web platforms, and local AI model inference on consumer GPUs.

What AI projects has Emilio Fonseca developed?

He has built verified AI implementations including Local LLM Inference on 4 GB VRAM (INT4 GPTQ quantization running 7B models at 23.4 tokens/sec on consumer GPUs), CORA (omnichannel conversational AI assistant with vector RAG), Dexter (autonomous multi-agent ecosystem with 44+ skills), Dino AI (reinforcement learning with computer vision), and the World Cup 2026 Predictor (XGBoost and Monte Carlo simulations).

In which locations and work arrangements does Emilio Fonseca operate?

Based in Tampico, Tamaulipas, Mexico. He delivers on-site and remote development for clients in Monterrey, Mexico City, Guadalajara, and across Mexico, as well as worldwide remote software engineering contracts.

What is an Offline-First Web App and why is it a specialty of Emilio Fonseca?

Offline-First Progressive Web Apps (such as MyAccountant and Vértice) run 100% on the client device without requiring cloud connectivity. They leverage local encrypted storage via IndexedDB, SQLite WASM, and Service Workers for zero cloud latency, zero external server fees, and complete cryptographic privacy.
